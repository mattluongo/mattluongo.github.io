I am a Research Physical Scientist at NOAA's [Pacific Marine Environmental Laboratory](https://www.pmel.noaa.gov/) in Seattle, WA. My goal as a climate dynamicist and large-scale physical oceanographer is to understand the ocean's role in driving past, present, and future climate variability and change through the lens of coupled ocean-atmosphere interactions. Check out my publications page for some recent studies.

I am a former CICOES Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the [University of Washington](https://environment.uw.edu/) and completed my PhD at [Scripps Institution of Oceanography](https://scripps.ucsd.edu/), UCSD as a NASA FINESST Graduate Research Fellow. Prior to that, I graduated from Harvard University in 2017 with a joint degree in Earth Science and Environmental Engineering and then worked as both a utility consultant and a research assistant for a land trust. In my free time you can find me exploring PNW natural spaces or catching live music.

Feel free to shoot me an email at matthew.luongo(at)noaa.gov or mluongo(at)uw.edu.
